WABOX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

WABOX helps a business owner process incoming WhatsApp Business customer text messages and organize extracted order details in a table that can be exported to Excel.

Data we process

Depending on the features you use, WABOX processes account identifiers, WhatsApp Business account identifiers, sender phone numbers, message text, extracted order summaries, subscription status and technical logs needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use data

Message content is processed to extract customer and order information requested by the account owner. Message data is stored to provide the live table, prevent duplicate processing, recover from temporary processing failures and support account operation.

Service providers

WABOX uses Meta WhatsApp Cloud API for message delivery, Groq for AI text processing, and Supabase for authentication, database and server functions. Google Play processes Android subscriptions. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Account data is retained while the account is active. You can request account deletion from the app settings. Deletion removes the WABOX account and associated records, subject to limited retention required by law, fraud prevention or transaction records.

Security

WABOX uses server-side access controls, encrypted storage for integration credentials, webhook signature verification and purchase-token deduplication. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

WABOX is a business productivity service and is not directed to children.
